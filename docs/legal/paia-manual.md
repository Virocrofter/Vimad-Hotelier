## 1. Purpose

Manual in terms of the Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000 (PAIA), read with POPIA.

## 2. Requesting access

- Requests may be made in writing (email is fine) to the Information Officer.
- Describe the record(s) sought with enough detail to locate them; attach proof of identity.
- Response within 30 days (extendable once, with notice, for large/voluminous requests).
- Fees: reproduction costs as prescribed; the first hour of search is free, thereafter the prescribed rate. Deposits may be required for large requests.

## 3. Categories of records

- Corporate records (CIPC filings, contracts).
- Client property records (operational data of subscribing properties — access for the property itself via the service's export; third-party requests require authority).
- Enquiry and lead records (the data subject's own details — provided directly to the requester after identity verification).
- Financial records (invoices, statements) as retained for tax law.

## 4. Grounds for refusal

Access may be refused only on grounds listed in PAIA (privacy of third parties, legal privilege, commercial harm, safety, etc.), with written reasons and the right to appeal to the Information Regulator.

## 5. Availability

This manual is available on request and is published with the company's privacy policy.
